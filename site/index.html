How the numbers are counted

Assignee
Column P. Where two names are joined by /, the later name owns the comment.
Severity
Column M: High, Medium or Low. Rows marked OK, or with no assignee, are left out.
Closed
Status is Closed, or the modeller response reads updated, completed, ignore or ignored.
On hold
Modeller response is RDB or HOLD. Hold items are counted in Overall Open.

Ready for issue

A document is ready to issue once every comment against it — column B, any assignee — is closed. One open or on-hold point keeps it back. By document, not by person, lot by lot.
